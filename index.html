plexlinux-backup

A personal backup tool that uses rclone to store and retrieve the owner’s backups in Google Drive. This application is for the owner’s use only and does not offer public registration.

Privacy policy

Privacy policy

plexlinux-backup uses Google Drive access to transfer and manage the owner’s backup files. Google account authorization is used only to perform these backup operations.

The application does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

The owner can revoke the application’s access through their Google Account permissions. Revoking access stops future authorized operations but does not delete existing backups; those can be deleted separately from Google Drive.
